A clear brief is the fastest way to get accurate quotes and avoid scope surprises. This template walks you through everything an agency needs to know—in plain language, no technical knowledge required.

**Inside:** business goals, target customers, required pages, content checklist, integrations (payments, WhatsApp, booking), technical ownership, timeline and budget range.
